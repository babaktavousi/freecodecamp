Capture settings

Spacing sets the stereo baseline. At a normal walking pace 0.3–0.5 s gives roughly half a metre between viewpoints, which suits indoor scenes; open outdoor scenes want more.

Depth is searched over this many hypotheses. More layers resolve distant surfaces more finely and cost time in proportion.

A single moving camera fixes shape but not size. The camera height sets the scale, so measurements come out in real metres.
